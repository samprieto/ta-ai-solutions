Business & Workforce OS

NorthStar

Turn business strategy into the organization, capabilities, workforce, and investment required to win.

What it does

The problem: Business strategy, workforce planning, organizational design, skills, budgets, and hiring are typically managed across disconnected systems, spreadsheets, presentations, and teams. Leaders define ambitious business goals but often lack a single view of whether they have the organizational capabilities, skills, structure, budget, and talent required to execute them.

This fragmentation makes fundamental questions surprisingly difficult to answer: Do we have the capabilities required to deliver our strategy? Where are our biggest workforce gaps? Is our organization structured effectively? What will our workforce plan cost? What happens if we restructure a team, move roles, invest in a new capability, or change our hiring plan?

The solution: NorthStar is a Business & Workforce Operating System that connects business strategy, organizational capabilities, workforce planning, financial planning, talent intelligence, and hiring execution in one integrated platform. Leaders start with where the business is going, and NorthStar helps translate those goals into the capabilities, skills, organizational structure, workforce investment, and talent strategy required to get there.

Business Impact

Before

Business strategy, workforce planning, organizational design, skills, financial planning, and recruiting operate across disconnected systems. Leaders rely on spreadsheets, presentations, HR systems, Finance models, ATS data, and manual analysis to understand their workforce.

Workforce planning frequently becomes a headcount budgeting exercise rather than a strategic discussion about the capabilities the company needs. Organizational changes are evaluated primarily through headcount and cost, with limited visibility into skills, capabilities, structure, talent risk, and future hiring requirements.

After

NorthStar creates a shared operating environment where executives, business leaders, Finance, People, and Talent teams plan the organization together. Business goals translate into Capabilities, Skills, Organization, Positions, People, Investment, Talent Strategy, and Execution.

Leaders can model decisions before making them and immediately understand their impact across the organization. The result is a different workforce-planning conversation. Not simply "How many people can we afford?" but "What organization and capabilities do we need to execute our strategy, and what is the smartest way to build them?"
